# Evidence support and regression for research findings

Design baseline `b0d3fdb1bb37d0e7f74dd1687c6421617ba29f5f`. The first deliverable is a reproducible local quality evaluation for the report-generation part of `generic-evidence-report@1`. An operator can register a suspect finding, inspect what its citations support, compare two generation policies on the same material, and retain or reject the candidate with a reason.

## Existing boundaries

The [findings reader](../../reference/research-findings-cli.md) returns the accepted questions, candidate statements, bound references, canonical artifact and same-run Evidence. [Application-owned finalization](../../decisions/research-findings-delivery-authority.md) checks source identity and exact excerpts. Opposite statements can cite the same genuine passage and both pass that structural check. The [v1 regression gate](../../reference/agent-evaluation-regression-gate.md), [v2 sensitivity gate](../../reference/agent-evaluation-sensitivity-gate.md) and [reviewed evolution cases](../../reference/evidence-gated-loop-kernel.md) retain their existing contracts.

This evaluation reads delivered artifacts or explicitly identified historical extracts. It writes versioned evaluation files. A score has no write access to delivery state, citation status, independent Evidence verification, the production prompt, or an accepted baseline.

## Case intake and dataset

- Use a saved validated findings CLI JSON as the normal intake. Record run, question, finding, report hash, the actual statement, **all** bound references and their exact spans in their frozen snippets. Preserve question-level dispositions, including unresolved questions with zero findings. An operator can register one finding as suspect and attach an expected behavior and reason. Automated signals including zero source calls, broken binding, missed answerable point and reviewer disagreement enter triage with their observation basis. A known-completed source call count of zero and an unobserved count have different meanings.
- Use independent axes for origin (actual local run, historical review, synthetic control), label authority (deterministic structural result, model proposal, human review), and diagnosis (confirmed, hypothesis, unobserved). Preserve reviewer and review date for human judgments. Retain a normal control for a reported failure. Deduplicate stable case identities without collapsing two versions of the same report into one observation. A receipt or description with a missing primary artifact stays usable as a historical pointer, outside the replayable natural-error count.
- Seed 4–6 task families and about 16–20 claim/evidence examples. Reuse suitable fixed sources, questions and controls from the earlier pilot when their contents and source identities can be verified. Add independent public material for holdout. Split by shared source and task family before tuning; a paraphrase, flipped claim and its original belong together. Old controls already examined count as development data. Once used for tuning, retire that holdout for future comparisons. Only distributable frozen material and its provenance enter the public benchmark. A fresh clone must replay without the historical branch or any private archive.
- Provide a small blind review sheet, targeting 6–8 varied claims. It exposes the question, statement, all cited passages and source versions. The reviewer records an independent judgment before seeing the Judge's answer. Only actually reviewed rows enter human calibration statistics. The operator can finish the entire offline workflow with explicit pending human judgments.

## Scoring and attribution

| Verdict | Decision on the statement and its own citations |
| --- | --- |
| Supported | Its cited passages jointly support every material part, including scope, time, version and qualification. Paraphrase is allowed. |
| Contradicted | A comparable cited passage explicitly refutes a material assertion. |
| Insufficient evidence | A part lacks support, the version is inapplicable, or the evidence cannot decide the assertion. |
| Conflicting evidence | Comparable cited passages directly support and oppose the same assertion. A statement accurately describing that disagreement can itself be supported. |

Record the evidence reference(s) for each material part and a short rationale. An uncited source that would support an answer can inform diagnosis only. Broken references are structural errors. Judge timeout, invalid response, absent report and unstarted task are distinct outcomes outside the four semantic classes. Also score answerable key points independently of how many findings the model produced, plus justified unresolved questions, erroneous abstentions and new material claims. Preserve the planned-task denominator alongside the delivered-and-scorable denominator.

Diagnose the earliest evidenced deviation along source retrieval, capture, transfer into the coordinator, generation, application binding and evaluator. Link only observed steps. On a public evaluation fixture the runner may retain the minimal passage actually visible at the generation call and its locator/hash. A hash or matching source ID alone cannot show that a qualifier survived transfer. Distinguish a newly observed replay from the original run. Application run/segment IDs and observed call/span IDs remain separate. Existing [closed telemetry](../../reference/observation-contract.md) and optional [LangSmith tracing](../../observability.md) remain diagnostic. The local workflow works with tracing disabled, and should not send raw prompts, source text, token credentials or provider errors to a trace backend.

## Two experiments and the decision

1. **Judge calibration.** Give the Judge the question, claim and frozen cited material. Hide the human label, A/B identity, other arm, answer key and expected verdict. Freeze prompt and model identity. Compare only completed predictions with human-reviewed examples. Report a four-class confusion table, false alarms on supported controls, false passes on unsupported assertions, actual denominators, unavailable results and disagreements. Re-scoring a retained output measures the evaluator.
2. **Generation A/B.** Use the normal coordinator's final-generation boundary at which native file tools can write the structured candidate. Freeze public pre-generation context, model configuration, tools and source bytes, keeping immutable identities. A uses current production instructions. B changes only the instruction to preserve object, version and conditions and name the missing evidence when qualification is impossible. Run both actual outputs through the same frozen-ledger binder and readback. Use the same Judge or blinded manual rubric for both arms. A parity check must tie the A envelope and tool contract to the current native producer. If exact parity cannot be established, report a prompt experiment, keep generation impact unverified and finish the other work. No full research rerun is required for this scoped comparison.
3. **Decision.** Classify comparable pairs as both correct, only A correct, only B correct, or both incorrect with a preregistered task rubric. A task passes when its required answerable points are supported, required unresolved points are handled correctly, and it has no material unsupported assertion or wrong refusal. Review all regressions and newly asserted material claims. Record calls, input/output tokens, elapsed time, and pricing basis where available. Keep evaluator overhead separate. Adopt the candidate only if a traceable observed content failure improves, reviewed normal controls acquire no new serious errors or wrong refusals, and coverage and the predeclared budget are acceptable. Otherwise retain A or record insufficient evidence. Promotion to the production path is a separate decision following the result.

## Operator outcome

One offline command produces authoritative comparison JSON and a deterministic Markdown table. Rows lead to the case, run and question, finding or unresolved disposition, exact quote and source, earliest observable error, comparison and decision. Show planned, started, delivered and scorable totals, fixes, regressions, wrong refusals, missing observations and resource use. Keep raw receipts local. A reader can reproduce the report twice from the same bytes with no network or keys, trace one normal and one failed example, and register a new suspect finding for the next comparison.

Real Judge and generator calls require explicit opt-in and a separately approved envelope for the model, request count, input/output tokens, wall time, expense and stopping rule. An exhausted or failed run retains evidence and unfinished rows. The earlier end-to-end research pilot stays closed. No dashboard service, new online gate, general replay platform, or hosted telemetry is required for this slice.
